\chapter*{Appendix A: Selected Platform Capabilities}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Appendix A: Selected Platform Capabilities}
\markboth{Appendix A}{Appendix A}

The orchestrator exposes a tenant-scoped set of capabilities consumed by the agent dashboard, the desktop client and the automation service. Every request is authenticated either with an end-user token or, for service-to-service calls, with a signed API key. Table~\ref{tab:api-endpoints} lists the most frequently invoked capabilities; the full surface area is documented in the orchestrator's internal API specification.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Selected platform capabilities exposed by the orchestrator.}
\label{tab:api-endpoints}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l X}
\toprule
\textbf{Capability} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
List cases             & Paginated case listing for the logged-in agent, with filter and sort options. \\
Get case detail        & Case detail with progressive sub-resource loading. \\
Submit disposition     & Triggers workflow advancement and pushes the next case to the agent dashboard. \\
Get case timeline      & Append-only event log: calls, messages, payments, state changes. \\
Create campaign        & Create a campaign from a validated CSV preview. \\
Preview campaign CSV   & Parse and validate an uploaded CSV without persisting. \\
Start campaign         & Move a scheduled campaign to the ongoing state. \\
Pause campaign         & Pause an in-flight campaign. \\
Get campaign metrics   & Live per-campaign counters streamed to the dashboard. \\
Save workflow          & Persist a tenant workflow definition. \\
Get workflow           & Fetch a workflow definition for editing or inspection. \\
Initiate AI call       & Manually trigger an AI voice call for a case. \\
Voice-platform callback & Inbound terminal callback from the internal voice AI platform. \\
WhatsApp callback      & Inbound delivery and reply notifications from the automation service. \\
SMS callback           & Inbound SMS delivery and reply notifications from the Android sender. \\
Pull next case         & Speed dialer: hand the next case to the current agent. \\
List QA samples        & Five-percent random sample of AI calls for QA audit. \\
Submit QA verdict      & Confirm or override the disposition recorded for a sampled call. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\chapter*{Appendix B: Campaign Upload Schema}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Appendix B: Campaign Upload Schema}
\markboth{Appendix B}{Appendix B}

Campaigns are seeded from a spreadsheet uploaded by the campaign manager. Each row corresponds to one case in the campaign. Table~\ref{tab:csv-schema} documents the fields the upload pipeline expects and validates.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Campaign upload schema.}
\label{tab:csv-schema}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l l c X}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Required} & \textbf{Validation} \\
\midrule
Customer name      & text    & yes & Non-blank, up to 100 characters. \\
Phone number       & text    & yes & International dialling format; duplicates within the file are removed. \\
Loan account number & text   & yes & Must match the tenant's expected pattern. \\
Principal amount   & number  & yes & Positive, two decimal places. \\
EMI amount         & number  & yes & Positive, not greater than the principal amount. \\
Overdue amount     & number  & yes & Positive, not less than the EMI amount. \\
Days past due      & integer & yes & Between 1 and 365; out-of-range rows are rejected. \\
Bucket             & label   & yes & One of the standard bucket labels; cross-checked against the days-past-due value. \\
Preferred language & label   & no  & Standard language code; defaults to the tenant default. \\
Promise-to-pay date & date   & no  & A future date in standard ISO format. \\
Tags               & text    & no  & Free-form comma-separated values; surfaces as filter chips on the dashboard. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
